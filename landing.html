KNOW WHAT TO EXPECT

Useful answers, before you start.

What do I need?

A Windows computer, Google Chrome, Internet and your own university account. Python is included. The app runs on your computer; the server provides shared AI access.

Which platforms work?

McGraw Hill has a dedicated adapter. Standard question controls on Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Pearson and Cengage are experimental. Custom drag-and-drop, special editors and automatic navigation outside McGraw may need your help. Compatibility is not universal.

Does it guarantee correct answers?

No. AI can make mistakes. More reasoning may take longer and does not guarantee accuracy. Review the session and use the assistant in accordance with your course rules.

How does the trial end?

It ends after 20 analyses or 48 hours, whichever comes first. There is no automatic charge. You can choose a paid plan afterward. The trial is limited to two activations per network per day and 100 trial analyses per day across the service.

Where does my information go?

Questions, images and descriptions used for analysis pass through the Study Pilot server and OpenAI. Your university sign-in happens in Chrome. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information in questions.

Why does Chrome open a blank tab?

Open your Canvas activity link in the new Chrome window. The illustrated guide shows where to paste it. The desktop app opens a welcome screen with instructions for opening your activity.
