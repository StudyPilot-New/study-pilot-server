YOUR FIRST SESSION, STEP BY STEP

Let’s get you started.

Windows + Google Chrome + your university account. Follow these four steps in order.

01 · Your account02 · Install03 · Your activity04 · Your pace

01 / YOUR ACCOUNT

Start with a free trial.

  1. Open My account and create an account with your email and password.
  2. Press Start my free trial: 20 analyses, available for 48 hours. No card or automatic charge.
  3. Use that same email and password in Mi cuenta → Entrar inside the desktop app.

Illustration of the flow. Desktop labels are shown as they appear in the app.

“Acceso activo” means you can continue. If access is pending or your password is incorrect, the message appears above the fields. Don’t share your password or access code.

02 / INSTALL ONCE

One installer. Python included.

  1. Download StudyPilotC-Setup.exe on your Windows computer.
  2. Close any older Study Pilot window. Open the installer and press Instalar.
  3. Press Abrir Study Pilot C, or use the new desktop shortcut.

Google Chrome must already be installed. You don’t need to install Python or paste an API key. This installer is not digitally signed; Windows may display a publisher warning.

03 / OPEN YOUR ACTIVITY

A new Chrome. Your own login.

  1. Copy the activity link from Canvas or your university platform.
  2. Paste it into Inicio rápido in the app and press Abrir actividad.
  3. In the new Chrome, sign in with your university account and complete any verification yourself.
  4. Open the activity’s questions. Keep this Chrome window open.

Already seeing about:blank? Paste your activity link into that Chrome address bar. The new Chrome has a separate login session from your usual browser. Use the Canvas activity link if it launches McGraw for you.

04 / KEEP THE SESSION VISIBLE

Choose a comfortable pace.

  1. In Actividad, choose Por pregunta to set seconds per question, or Duración total for an estimate.
  2. Your changes apply during the session. For example, 180 questions at 40 seconds is about 120 minutes.
  3. On Canvas, Next is clicked after the selected answers are verified and your pacing wait ends. Final quiz submission stays manual. Watch the status: analyzing, waiting, sent or needs attention. Use Pausar, Recuperar or Detener when needed.

Analysis and retries can take longer. AI can be wrong; review its work. The trial counts analyses, including corrections, rather than completed questions.

If something does not move

I pressed Entrar and nothing changed

Install the latest download. The result appears above the email field. Active access takes you to Actividad. Use Comprobar mi acceso guardado to check an existing login.

Chrome is open but no questions are detected

Make sure you opened the questions in the Chrome launched by the app. Complete the university login. If questions are visible, use Recuperar and inspect Actividad’s log.

The trial or payment is pending

Activate the trial in My account, or wait for the administrator to verify a payment. Payments do not activate access automatically. After approval, sign in again or check your saved access.

I forgot my password

Contact the administrator. Automatic email password recovery is not available yet. Never send your old password.
